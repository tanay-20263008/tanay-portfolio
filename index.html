How It Works

From first call
to live system

Every system is built specifically for your business. No templates, no shortcuts. You talk directly to me throughout — not a project manager, not a team. Just me.

1
Day 1

Discovery Call

30–60 minutes. I ask deep questions about your workflow — not what you want built, but what problems you face every morning. I map every pain point before touching code.

2
Day 1 – 3

Architecture & Design

I design every screen, every workflow, every feature. You get a clear scope and approve everything before a single line of code is written. No surprises at the end.

3
Day 3 – 25

Build & Refine

I build fast with daily updates. You see the system taking shape in real time. Feedback is immediate — changes happen same day. You always know exactly where things stand.

4
Week 3 – 4

Live Deployment

Your system goes live. I walk your team through every feature, handle all training, and hand over complete ownership — source code, database, documentation. 100% yours forever.

Delivery timelines: Custom management systems (hospitals, coaching institutes, real estate, gyms, factories) are delivered in 3–4 weeks from advance payment. Professional websites and booking systems are live in ~6 days. Both are one-time payment — no subscriptions, no monthly charges, ever.
